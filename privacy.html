Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 September 2026

In short

  • Right now we only collect your email address when you join the waitlist.
  • We use it to tell you when Folio launches. We don't sell it or use it for advertising.
  • You can unsubscribe or ask us to delete it at any time.

1. Who we are

Folio is a net worth tracker for iPhone, currently in development. It is operated by Spencer Wong in Australia (“Folio”, “we”, “us”). This policy explains how we handle personal information through this website and the Folio waitlist.

We follow the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Spam Act 2003 (Cth).

2. What we collect

When you join the waitlist, we collect:

  • your email address
  • that you agreed to hear from us, and when
  • which sign-up form you used (for example, the top or bottom of the page)

When you visit the website, our hosting provider and font provider automatically receive standard technical information, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. This is needed to deliver the website to you.

We don't use advertising trackers, and this website doesn't set cookies. We don't ask for your name, bank details, broker logins or information about your investments.

3. How we use it

We use your email address to:

  • tell you when Folio is available and send occasional updates about its launch
  • reply to you if you contact us

We use technical information to run the website, keep it secure and fix problems.

We never sell your personal information or share it with advertisers.

4. Emails and unsubscribing

We only email you if you joined the waitlist. Every email includes an unsubscribe link that works without logging in. We'll stop emailing you within five working days of your request.

5. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run Folio. They can only use your information to provide their service to us:

  • Website hosting: GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which receives technical information such as your IP address when you visit
  • Waitlist and email: [waitlist provider to be confirmed]
  • Fonts: Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser loads the website's fonts

Some of these providers store data outside Australia, including in the United States. We choose providers that protect personal information to a standard comparable to Australian law.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

6. How we keep it safe and how long we keep it

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, including limiting who can access it and using providers with strong security practices.

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. If Folio doesn't launch, we'll delete the waitlist.

If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we'll tell you and, where required, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

7. Accessing, correcting or deleting your information

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. Contact us and we'll respond within 30 days. It's free.

8. The Folio app

The Folio app isn't available yet. Before it launches, we'll update this policy to explain what the app collects, such as your account details and the holdings you enter.

Holdings are sensitive financial information, and we'll treat them that way. We won't send them to third-party analytics, and you'll be able to delete your account and data from inside the app.

9. Complaints

If you're concerned about how we've handled your personal information, please contact us first. We'll respond within 30 days.

If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

10. Changes to this policy

We'll update this page when our practices change and update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your email address, we'll tell you by email first.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, message Spencer Wong on LinkedIn.

Folio is a tracking tool and doesn't provide financial advice.
